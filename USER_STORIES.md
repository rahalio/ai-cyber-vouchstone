# Vouchstone — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### CISO and assurance lead

- As a CISO, I want each domain of my posture statement to show its supporting evidence and that evidence's expiry date, so that I can sign an assurance statement knowing precisely which parts of it I could defend under challenge.
- As a CISO, I want a forward view of which evidence lapses in the next two quarters and what it will cost the score, so that I can commission exercises and attestations before the board sees a decline rather than after.
- As a CISO, I want the divergence between believed impact and actual control investment reported to me before it is reported to the committee, so that I can either correct the allocation or explain it in my own words.
- As an assurance lead, I want the same evidence base to generate the committee pack, the regulatory return, and the insurance proposal response, so that three documents describing one organisation stop contradicting each other.

### Security operations manager

- As a security operations manager, I want detection coverage expressed per named priority asset with the gaps listed, so that a coverage conversation is about specific systems rather than about tooling spend.
- As a security operations manager, I want time to detect calculated from our own incident timestamps and shown as a distribution, so that our slow tail is visible to me before a regulator finds it.
- As a security operations manager, I want to see how many of last year's confirmed incidents we found ourselves, so that I can argue for internal monitoring investment with a number the board cannot dismiss.

### Adversary emulation lead

- As an emulation lead, I want exercises scheduled against named priority assets with an independent provider and a recorded scope, so that a passing result means something specific was tested rather than that a test happened.
- As an emulation lead, I want an exercise that was not run by its due date to expire the associated evidence automatically, so that a deferred exercise has a visible consequence.
- As an emulation lead, I want each finding to carry a named remediation owner and to suppress the relevant domain score until closed, so that findings stop accumulating in a spreadsheet nobody reads.

### Business asset owner

- As a business asset owner, I want to attest to my priority assets and their location and to be asked to re-attest on a defined cycle, so that the register reflects a real estate rather than a snapshot taken during a project.
- As a business asset owner, I want to see the detection coverage and open findings on my own assets and nobody else's, so that I get the accountability without the whole firm's target list.

### Internal audit, insurance and board governance

- As an internal auditor, I want to test whether a published domain score was supported by valid evidence on the date it was published, so that I can audit the assurance process instead of re-performing the assessment.
- As an insurance and legal liaison, I want every claim in a proposal response traced to dated evidence, so that our representations to an underwriter are defensible and our cover is not voidable on a misrepresentation.
- As an audit and risk committee chair, I want to own the evidence decay rules and score caps, and to see every change to them logged with its approver, so that the security function cannot adjust the mechanism by which it is measured.
- As a committee secretary, I want an incident whose discoverer was not our security function to appear in the pack as such, so that the board learns how we find out about breaches and not only how many we had.
